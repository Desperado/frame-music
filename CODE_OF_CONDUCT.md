# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Frame Music's issues, pull requests and other project spaces is expected to treat people respectfully, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and give constructive feedback about the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, and publication of someone else's private information are unacceptable. Respect boundaries, stop behavior when asked, and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.

Report concerns privately to maintainer Ruslan Strazhnyk at strazhnyk@gmail.com. Share only the information needed to explain the incident. The maintainer will handle reports discreetly and may remove content, limit participation or ban participants depending on severity and repeated behavior. Enforcement applies to maintainers as well as other contributors. Good-faith reports and participation must not result in retaliation.
